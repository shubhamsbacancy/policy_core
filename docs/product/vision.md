# PolicyCore Product Vision

## Problem
Many insurers and MGAs still rely on legacy policy administration systems that are costly to configure, slow to change, and hard to integrate.

## Vision
PolicyCore is an API-first, cloud-native policy administration platform for property insurance teams that need to launch and iterate products quickly with modern developer ergonomics and AI-assisted workflows.

## Target Users
- Operations administrators
- Underwriters and service teams
- Independent agents and agency staff
- Policyholders in self-service flows

## North-Star Outcomes
- Shorten quote-to-bind cycle time.
- Increase underwriting consistency with explainable AI support.
- Improve customer and agent self-service completion rates.
- Deliver auditable lifecycle events for compliance and reporting.

## Product Principles
- API-first from day one.
- Multi-tenant by design, simple to seed/demo.
- Deterministic core rating with AI as decision support.
- Immutable pricing/policy versions for traceability.
- Security-by-default via RLS and strict key boundaries.
